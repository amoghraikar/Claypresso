# Shipping & Delivery Policy Route (`src/app/shipping/`)

Transparent documentation detailing Claypresso's domestic Indian shipping operations, dispatch timeframes, and courier partnerships.

---

## Content Highlights

- **Domestic Only Policy**: Explicit explanation of India-only delivery.
- **Shipping Tiers**:
  - Orders under ₹500: Flat ₹60 courier fee.
  - Orders ₹500 and above: Free domestic shipping.
- **Dispatch Times**:
  - Ready-Made Stock: Dispatched in 24–48 hours.
  - Made-to-Order Pieces: 7–10 days production before dispatch.
  - Custom Commissions: ~25 days creation time.
- **Courier Partners**: India Post (Speed Post) and DTDC with tracking links.
- **Transit Duration**: Typically 3–5 business days depending on city and postal zone.
