# Frequently Asked Questions Route (`src/app/faq/`)

Searchable, category-divided FAQ addressing customer inquiries on materials, durability, care, orders, and custom commissions.

---

## FAQ Categories

1. **Materials & Clay Care**: What polymer clay is, water resistance, drop durability, and cleaning tips.
2. **Orders & Payments**: Payment methods accepted (UPI, Cards, NetBanking), modifying orders, and receipts.
3. **Shipping & Delivery**: Tracking number timelines, courier queries, and packaging safety.
4. **Custom Orders & Commissions**: Uploading reference images, quote adjustments, and customization feasibility.
5. **Returns & Damaged Goods**: Studio policies regarding handcrafted transit breakages and replacements.
